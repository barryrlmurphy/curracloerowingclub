Race report · Indoor 23 February 2026 · Coach Walsh

Three medals in Limerick — and a J16 four that lit the place up.

Curracloe sent thirty-one rowers to the Irish Indoor Rowing Championships at the University of Limerick. Aoife Ní Bhriain took gold in W MAS D 1×; Jamie Kelly took silver in J18 1×; and the J16 four — Niamh, Saoirse, Conn and Eoin — pulled a 1:41.0 split to take bronze in their first national appearance. A long write-up follows below.
