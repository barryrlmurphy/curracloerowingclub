Frequently asked

Before your first session

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. We've members who came from couch-to-5k, from cycling, from no sport at all. The first six weeks are about technique, not power. The fitness comes.

What does it cost?

€120/year for adults, €60/year for juniors. Held flat thanks to sponsorship from Kinetic Financial Advice. First session is free.

What should I bring?

Trainers you don't mind getting damp, kit you can move in, a bottle of water, and a towel. We provide boats, blades, ergs, and a coach.

Is it safe?

Yes. All sessions follow Rowing Ireland safety guidelines. Lifejackets for novices, a launch on the water with the rowers, and a swim-test before any unaccompanied river session.
