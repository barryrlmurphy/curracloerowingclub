About the club

A community rowing club,
on three surfaces, in one corner of Wexford.

Founded in 2024 by a small group of beach- and river-rowers. We run a year-round programme for ages 12 to 70+ — river, coast, and the indoor erg shed.

Curracloe Rowing Club rows on three surfaces — the River Slaney, the open coast at Curracloe and Ballinesker beaches, and the indoor erg shed when the weather closes in. Membership runs from children to senior masters competitors; there's a boat and a session for everyone.

We send delegations to the Irish Indoor Rowing Championships in Limerick every winter, race the Slaney through the summer, and put on the 24-Hour Rowathon for local charities each February. We compete proudly across the country and punch well above our weight on the podium — but the club is built first and foremost on showing up for each other at six in the morning.

We're proudly sponsored by local businesses — chiefly Kinetic Financial Advice — which keeps registration fees accessible for every family in the area. Never rowed before? Good. We started somewhere too.

Club story

From beach-launch to podium, in eighteen months.

  • 2024
    Founded — May 2024 Twelve members. One borrowed eight, two ergs, and a beach launch at Ballinesker.
  • 2024
    First boathouse — October 2024 The Slaney Boathouse opens on the Ferrycarrig Road. Coaching team confirmed. Member count: 48.
  • 2025
    Inaugural 24-Hour Rowathon — February 2025 €4,820 raised for Relay For Life Wexford and the Irish Cancer Society.
  • 2025
    First Slaney Head race — August 2025 Niamh O'Connor takes gold in W Sen 1×; the Open 4× takes silver.
  • 2026
    Irish Indoor Championships, Limerick — February 2026 Three medals. Aoife Ní Bhriain — gold W MAS D 1×. Jamie Kelly — silver J18 1×. J16 4× — bronze.
