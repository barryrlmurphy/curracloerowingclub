Club News
April, May & June 2026

Curracloe Rowing Club took part in the 2026 World Rowing Virtual Series , an international challenge that ran from 1 April to 30 June and brought rowing clubs together from all over the world. The series was built around six monthly challenges, two set each month, giving members a fresh target to chase every couple of weeks.

Spreading the challenges out over three months turned out to be exactly what the club needed. Each new challenge kept training sharp and motivation high, and having a set target on the calendar made it easier to stay consistent through the spring months rather than letting training slide.

Six challenges, three months, and a club that stayed committed to every one of them. Thanks to everyone who logged their metres and kept the motivation going through April, May and June.
